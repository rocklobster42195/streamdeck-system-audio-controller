# Changelog

All notable changes to SA-C (System Audio Controller) are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- **One look for all keys (kit 0.1.0-alpha.36): one symbol size, no discs behind symbols, the hourglass replaces the symbol, shuffle is the thin crossed arrows (on) or the parallel arrows (off), and every error is the same red badge in the top left corner of a key.** (From a beta tester's remark that the keys looked like they belong to different plugins.)
- **Fixed: a skip no longer looks like a pause.** Between two tracks the app is silent for a moment while its session still says "playing"; the keys now keep showing "playing" for up to 2.5 s of silence (something that did play, an app with sound here). A real pause shows at once. A cover brought down to 256 px (kit 0.1.0-alpha.28) and the kit's smaller Panorama frames (alpha.28/29) make the keys lighter for Stream Deck.
- **Fixed: A new cover's colour reached the Panorama late: the colour fade took four seconds instead of one (kit 0.1.0-alpha.23).**
- **Fixed: a dial in a shared Panorama stayed black** after its plugin (or another one in the row) restarted, until the plugin leading the row restarted too (kit 0.1.0-alpha.7).

### Added
- **The Output dial shows its device's kind** (headphones, speakers, a monitor's speakers) as an icon in the middle of the Ring, Pie and Open ring, as the App dial shows its app.
- **Play/Pause key: double tap = next, triple tap = previous** (switch it on in the key's settings; the tap speed is adjustable). A single tap then waits a moment before it plays or pauses; with it off the key reacts at once.
- **Diagnostics in the settings window:** a collapsed field at the end with the end of the plugin's log (warnings and errors marked, a filter), a small copy icon at the top right that copies a report (versions of the plugin, Stream Deck and the other plugins, then the log; tokens, passwords and e-mail addresses hidden), "Show log file" (opens the file manager) and "Report an issue" (copies the report and opens the issue page).
- **Loading and failed commands show in the corner** of the Play/Pause, Playback Control and Volume keys and the Track dial: an hourglass at the bottom left while a command takes a while, a red mark for a few seconds when it was refused (the family's status badge, from the kit).
- **Track dial** (Stream Deck +): the family's dial (the same in Music Assistant Controller and Sonos Controller, from the kit). Cover, title, artist and progress of the music on the PC (Spotify, a browser tab, Media Player) or any other player on the deck; without a chosen player it follows the one that started last, apps included. Rotate to seek (5, 10, 15 or 30 s per tick), push for the next track, tap to play or pause. Track info or Equalizer, the cover left or right, and it takes part in its row's Panorama.
- **Playback Control** key: the family's key (the same in Music Assistant Controller and Sonos Controller, from the kit). Next, previous, shuffle and repeat for music on the PC (Spotify, a browser tab, Media Player) or any other player on the deck. Hold Next or Previous to seek instead of skipping (5, 10, 15 or 30 s per tap, quick taps add up). Toggles show their state at once.
- **Volume** key: the family's key (the same in Music Assistant Controller and Sonos Controller, from the kit). Louder, quieter, mute and a preset for an app on the PC (its volume in Windows' mixer) or any other player on the deck; the mute key as a ring, pie or open ring, and a preset volume that is "on" while the volume is at it.
- **Play/Pause** key: the family's key (the same in Music Assistant Controller and Sonos Controller, from the kit). Plays or pauses music on the PC (Spotify, a browser tab, Media Player) or any other player on the deck; without a chosen player the one that started last, apps included. Shows the cover, dimmed with a play symbol while paused, optionally the title, the progress and the player's name; two icons at the top to choose: your own icon, the app (its icon) or the battery.
- SA-C's players on deckbus carry their app as the source, with the app's icon (`source`, `sourceIcon`), for the keys' source corner in every plugin.
- **App Output** key: plays one or more apps on another device (Discord on the headset, Spotify on the speakers); each press moves them to the next device of your list (e.g. headset → speakers → Windows default), each with its own name, icon and colour (the kit's Multi-state key). Windows keeps it for the app; an app that is closed gets it when it starts. Uses Windows' internal app routing (as EarTrumpet does). Apps show up on whichever device they play (also the App dial and App Mute key, after a route), with the volume of the session that plays.
- **Calls for other plugins:** SA-C tells deckbus when the computer is in a call (`call`: Teams, Discord, Zoom …, with the app), so other plugins can pause or lower music or show it; from Windows' own call detection (what it lowers other sounds for), else a known call app using the mic. Also which apps use the mic now (`mic.users`).
- **Music on your PC for other plugins' keys:** Windows' media sessions (Spotify, a browser tab, Media Player …) are players on deckbus (`players`), with title, artist, position and cover (made square, without black bars, at most 300 px: Stream Deck stretches pictures to the key, so a YouTube picture looked squashed). Sonos Controller's and Music Assistant Controller's media keys can play/pause, skip, seek and set the volume of them (the volume is the app's volume in Windows' mixer). On by default; the global setting `othersMayControlPlayers` switches it off.
- **A closed app stays a player:** after you quit Spotify (or another app with music), its player stays on deckbus as closed (also after a restart), so a key bound to it keeps it; **Play starts the app and plays** (Store apps by their app id, programs by their file).
- The helper compiles with the C# compiler directly (instead of Add-Type), so it can use Windows' media sessions; on Windows before 1809 it runs without them.
- **Horseshoe** as a fifth look on the Output and App dials: the volume on a ring open at the bottom (from the kit, as in Home Assistant Controller and Sonos Controller).
- **Keys in the family look** (the kit's key style, as in Music Assistant Controller and Home Assistant Controller): large icons, bold names; the default output device is "on" with a tinted plate and frame. A press that can't do anything (app closed, device gone) shows the kit's alert instead of Stream Deck's. With a Stream Deck title on a key, SA-C draws no name of its own (they would overlap).
- **Panorama behind the Output and App dials** (from the kit): one effect for the whole row of dials, also across Music Assistant Controller's, X-Air Controller's and Home Assistant Controller's dials, chosen in the settings window with a tick per dial; only text and meters get a dark backing.
- **Short settings panels with a settings window** on the Output and App dials (as in the family): step, Panorama and Other plugins are in the window behind "More settings …".
- The Output and App dials draw with the kit's level strip (shared with X-Air Controller); they look exactly as before.
- **Output** dial (Windows): the default output device's volume (rotate) and mute (push or tap), with the level of what is playing in four looks: Digital, Classic (VU meters), Ring and Pie. Follows a change of the default device. Own name possible.
- **App** dial: one app's volume (Spotify, Firefox, Discord, …) with mute, the app's icon and its own level, in the same four looks. Greyed out with its last volume while the app is closed.
- **App Mute** key: mutes one app; its icon large, a small level, red while muted, grey while closed.
- App icons come from the programs (also in the settings' app list) and are kept, so they show while an app is closed.
- **Microphone** key: mutes the default mic, clearly red while muted; otherwise a small level (while an app records).
- **Output Device** key: makes a device the default, or switches between two (e.g. headphones and speakers); icon from the name, framed while active.
- A small helper for Windows Core Audio (PowerShell with a C# file, readable in the plugin folder), started once and restarted if it stops.
- deckbus: the mic's level is marked as an input (`input: true` in `streams`), and Windows' system sounds are not offered as a level, so lists like Music Assistant Controller's "Moves with" stay short.
- **Ducking** for other plugins on deckbus, only those you allow (settings: "Other plugins", off by default): they may lower the output device (in real dB) or one app for a while; SA-C restores the exact volume afterwards, also when the plugin leaves or loses its permission. A volume you set in the meantime stays.
- **deckbus:** SA-C tells other plugins whether the system audio is available and offers levels (output `meters/system-output`, mic `meters/system-input`, every app `meters/app-<name>`), each sent only while another plugin uses it (e.g. Music Assistant Controller's Panorama); shares the mic's muted state (`mic`) and where its actions are (`actions`).
